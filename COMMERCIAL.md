## What the AGPL already covers

- Personal use needs no license. Download it, run it and modify it for yourself at no cost.
- If you distribute Lodestar or a modified version, the AGPL requires you to provide the corresponding source under the same license.
- The AGPL also covers network use: if you run a modified version as a service that other people use over a network, you must offer them its source under the AGPL.

## When you need a commercial license

A commercial license is for proprietary redistribution: embedding Lodestar in a closed-source product, shipping it inside an application whose source you do not publish, or running a hosted service based on it without releasing your source under the AGPL.
